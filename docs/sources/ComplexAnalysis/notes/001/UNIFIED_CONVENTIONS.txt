D12 UNIFIED CONVENTIONS AND PROOF BOUNDARIES

1. Omega denotes an open subset of C. A domain is nonempty, open and connected.
   Empty and disconnected variants are explicitly stated when used. Do not
   replace connectedness by path connectedness without the open-plane bridge.
2. Holomorphic means complex differentiable at every point. Real C1, complex
   differentiability at a point, and a local convergent complex power series
   start as distinct notions. Goursat/Cauchy/Taylor prove the relevant equivalence.
3. A conformal map is locally holomorphic with nonzero derivative; a conformal
   equivalence is a global biholomorphism. Injectivity and nonzero derivative
   are not silently interchangeable before the local multiplicity theorem.
4. A path is a continuous map on a compact real interval, with piecewise C1
   regularity for the basic contour integral. A finite cycle is an integral
   linear combination of closed piecewise C1 paths. Constant/degenerate paths
   have zero integral. Integral depends on traversal, not only trace. Rectifiable
   extensions use the proved BV/Stieltjes bridge, not an unjustified derivative.
5. ind(Gamma,a)=(2 pi i)^(-1) integral_Gamma dz/(z-a). Points stay off the trace.
   Homotopy and zero index on the complement are distinct hypotheses; prove the
   respective Cauchy formulations and their implications. Do not assume Jordan
   separation for arbitrary self-intersecting paths.
6. Taylor/Laurent expansion domains, radii, compact subannuli and zero/pole
   multiplicities are explicit. Identically zero and constant maps are separate.
7. Scalar meromorphic functions exclude identically infinity. Holomorphic maps
   into the sphere may include that constant. Infinity chart is w=1/z; residue
   of a differential at infinity includes the Jacobian minus sign.
8. Finite normality in H(Omega) uses locally uniform ordinary complex convergence.
   Spherical normality in meromorphic maps allows the constant infinity. The
   constants n demonstrate why local boundedness is not equivalent to the latter.
9. Harmonic initially means real-valued C2 with Laplacian zero. Prove mean-value,
   regularity, conjugate and holomorphic bridges. Harmonic conjugate differential
   is -u_y dx+u_x dy. Global conjugates require zero periods.
10. Use continuous subharmonic comparison functions in the chosen Perron class.
    Bounded domains and continuous boundary data are explicit. Boundary
    attainment requires barriers/regularity; RMT alone supplies no boundary map.
11. Plane Runge pole sets meet every component of the spherical complement.
    At infinity a pole means polynomial terms. Exhaustions and compact tails
    are proved. Products have locally finite divisors with exact multiplicities.
12. General-domain Weierstrass uses a valid boundary-factor construction; do not
    exponentiate an ML primitive unless every noncontractible period is controlled.
13. Germs have representatives near their basepoint. Monodromy requires actual
    continuation along every path of the homotopy, or all paths in the simply
    connected-domain corollary. A local homeomorphism is not automatically a
    covering. Supply the path/homotopy lifting bridges actually used.
14. Riemann surfaces are Hausdorff, second countable, connected complex
    one-manifolds. Chart independence, compact-map degree and branching are
    proved. Future D23 and D22 are ownership links, not available proof axioms.
15. Lattice Lambda=Z omega1+Z omega2 with Im(omega2/omega1) nonzero; normalize
    orientation positive when using oriented boundary integrals. Weierstrass
    p(z)=1/z^2+sum_{lambda!=0}((z-lambda)^(-2)-lambda^(-2));
    g2=60 sum lambda^(-4), g3=140 sum lambda^(-6), so
    p'^2=4p^3-g2 p-g3. Absolute convergence/reindexing and true periods are proved.
16. Cubic coordinates [Z:X:Y] satisfy Y^2 Z=4 X^3-g2 X Z^2-g3 Z^3. Torus map
    z -> [1:p(z):p'(z)], lattice class -> [0:0:1]. Prove nonsingularity,
    infinity charts, holomorphic inverse and group-law comparison, not a set
    bijection alone. No converse uniformization of arbitrary cubics is claimed.
17. Bloch/Schottky/Picard need actual proofs, separate from locally bounded
    Montel. Use any safe proved universal constant; no historical sharpness claim.
18. Selected scope is recorded in UNIFIED_RESULT_LEDGER.json. Gamma/zeta and
    complex ODE application branches, full Jordan/prime-end boundary theory,
    general surface uniformization/RR/duality and full modular forms are deferred.
    This is not a claim to reproduce every result in five books.
19. Adopted main results retain all hypotheses and conclusions. Equivalent valid
    proofs and exact previously proved prerequisites are permitted. Unused
    source-route helper steps are not new deliverables; a necessary lemma must
    still be proved or matched to a precise legitimate prior foundation.
20. Original prose/proofs, consistent notation and exact source comparison only;
    do not copy textbook exposition. Source theorem omissions, exercises and
    names are not proof certificates. Record discovered errors and resolved gaps.
