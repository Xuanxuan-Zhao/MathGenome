\chapter*{Purpose and reading contract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Purpose and reading contract}
These notes organize the undergraduate abstract-algebra direction of the
MathGenome curriculum. They give one mathematical development rather than
concatenating ten textbook syllabi. The expanded inventory has
408 stable knowledge identifiers in 23 modules.
It preserves the reviewed 318-entry core and adds coherent supplements
selected by a complete ten-book chapter/appendix coverage audit.
A knowledge identifier locates a family of mathematical obligations; it
does not prescribe a Lean-file boundary or future worker-task count.

The core retains its proof-based order: rings and PID modules precede
abelian-group classification, and Galois theory precedes representations.
The supplements extend groups, polynomials, fields, characters, modules and
division rings. The algebraic FTA appendix uses earlier algebra and real
IVT. Forward proof references must not create dependency cycles.

Each result retains its hypotheses, coefficient objects, chosen embeddings
and boundary conventions. Isomorphic objects are compared by explicit
structure-preserving maps. Existence, normalization and uniqueness are
separate claims. A reference means the actual stated theorem or a declared
foundation, not a merely similar topic name.

\paragraph{Coverage and subject boundaries.}
The 153-record audit accounts for all ten books' chapters and appendices,
not every exercise. D05 linear algebra is reused. Full computational
algebra, geometry, extended arithmetic, coding and cryptography retain
named subject routes. D22 is advanced \emph{undergraduate}; routing does
not claim its notes exist. The remaining-scope ledger preserves these
open topics. Homological algebra and specialized infinite/differential
Galois theory remain outside this manuscript.

\paragraph{Relation to formalization.}
This is a mathematical input manuscript, not a Lean library. The fixed
MathGenome v0.1.1/Mathlib environment can supply formal results only after
their exact statements and interfaces are checked. The earlier preparation
observed 375 candidate declaration types for the original core. Newly added
entries do not inherit those API checks. Source fidelity, mathematical
proof review and Lean kernel acceptance remain distinct stages.

\paragraph{Source corrections.}
Original textbook files are preserved. The notes record rather than copy
false or unguarded assertions: central-series comparisons, polynomial
Fermat after common-factor cancellation, and resultant specialization
after degree loss need particular care. General multiplication-map
norm/trace is distinguished from distinct-embedding formulas, which need
separability. The main ring track is associative and unital; division
rings are not assumed commutative. Proof corrections and author-derived
support are identified in the source and audit records.
