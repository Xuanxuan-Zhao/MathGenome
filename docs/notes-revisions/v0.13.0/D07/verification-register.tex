\chapter{Verification boundary and formal reuse}
\label{app:verification}
The audit distinguishes mathematical proof review from identifier coverage,
finite examples and typesetting checks. Neither a citation nor a successful
PDF build is treated as a proof.

\section{Explicit foundations}
Chapter~\ref{chap:foundations} declares the logical, linear-algebra and
analytic foundations. Complex algebraic closedness is proved in
Appendix~\ref{app:algebraic-fta}, without an external FTA premise.
Appendix~\ref{app:pi-transcendence} proves its special transcendence theorem
using the declared elementary exponential laws, Euler identity and real
calculus. No external transcendence theorem is assumed.

\section{Scope of the expanded review}
The original seventeen module files and pi proof retain their reviewed
mathematical text. All six supplements and the algebraic FTA proof receive
author checks and independent cross-review, bound to final file hashes.
Source corrections and author-derived generalizations remain explicit.
The remaining-scope ledger preserves source topics assigned to other
undergraduate directions; it does not claim their notes already exist.
